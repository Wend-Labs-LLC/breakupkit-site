If you're in crisis
Call or text 988 (988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, US) or text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line). Both are free and open 24/7. If you're in immediate danger, call 911.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your phone. Break-Up Kit has no accounts and no servers of its own. Check-ins, Spin Check history, mantras, the counter and SendEx messages never leave your device. The Privacy Policy lists the little the app does send: an anonymous purchase record, and requests for its fonts.
What's free, and what does it cost?
Spin Check, Mantras, the daily check-in, the counter and the More support page are free forever. SendEx, Funny Bone and Light Mode come with a 7-Day free trial, then the Full Unlock opens them for good with one $4.99 purchase. There's no subscription.
How does the 7-Day free trial work?
Tap any of the three tools, then Start the free 7-day trial. It costs nothing, and you can start it once. It runs for 7 days from the day you start it, and the app shows how many days are left. When it ends, those three tools lock again. You're never charged automatically. They stay locked until you choose the $4.99 Full Unlock.
I bought the Full Unlock. How do I get it on a new phone?
Open Break-Up Kit, tap SendEx, Funny Bone or Light Mode, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Your purchase transfers, but your notes and history don't, because they only ever lived on your old phone.
My daily reminder isn't showing up.
Check that notifications are allowed for Break-Up Kit in your phone's Settings, then turn the reminder off and on again in the app.
Where did my SendEx text go?
Texts and emails rest on your shelf for 24 hours, then fade for good unless you tap Keep another day. Faded messages can't be recovered by anyone, including us.
How do I delete everything?
Delete the app. That removes all of your data, because none of it is stored anywhere else. You can also delete call recordings, shelf messages and Spin Check history inside the app.
Is this therapy?
No. Break-Up Kit is a self-help tool for practice and reflection. It isn't medical care, therapy or crisis support. The app's More support page lists crisis lines and ways to find a therapist.
