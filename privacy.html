Privacy Policy

Break-Up Kit, by Wend Labs LLC

Effective November 3, 2026.

The short version. We don't collect, store or see anything you put into Break-Up Kit. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything you write, record or track stays on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is an anonymous purchase record, used to unlock and restore the trial or unlock (see Purchases).

Who we are

Break-Up Kit is made by Wend Labs LLC. Contact: keith@wendlabs.net.

What we collect

Nothing you put into the app. Wend Labs doesn't collect personal information, usage data, location or diagnostics from the app, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking code. The one exception is the anonymous purchase record described under Purchases.

What stays on your phone

The app saves these only in its own storage on your device:

  • Daily check-ins and the optional word you add
  • Spin Check ratings, notes and history
  • Mantras you save or write
  • The counter label and start date you choose
  • SendEx texts and emails, which fade after 24 hours unless you keep them
  • SendEx call recordings, which stay until you delete them
  • Your reminder time and app settings

Photos and videos you attach in SendEx are shown on screen and never saved. You pick them with your phone's own photo picker, so the app only sees the ones you choose and never gets access to your photo library. Deleting the app deletes all of this data. If your phone backs up apps to iCloud or another service you control, that backup may include this data under that service's terms.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only to record SendEx calls you start. Recordings stay on your phone.
  • Camera: only to take a picture or video to attach to a SendEx message. Nothing is saved.
  • Photos: the app doesn't ask for access to your photo library. When you attach a photo or video in SendEx, your phone's own picker shares only the items you choose. Nothing is saved.
  • Notifications: only for the optional daily reminder, which is scheduled on your phone.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's Settings.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are paid through Apple. Wend Labs never receives your name, Apple Account, email or payment details.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm purchases, restore them and show the current price. When the app starts, it contacts RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID that it creates (we don't connect it to who you are)
  • purchase records from the App Store (product, price, date, transaction ID)
  • technical details that come with any internet request, such as IP address, device model, operating system, app version and language or region

This is used only to unlock and restore your purchase. It isn't used for advertising or tracking, and we don't sell it. Nothing you write, record or track in the app is sent. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

To ask us to delete the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat, email us. Deleting it may stop Restore purchases from working.

Links to other services

The More support page links to outside services such as the 988 Lifeline, Crisis Text Line, Psychology Today, Open Path Collective, BetterHelp, NAMI and 7 Cups. They aren't run by Wend Labs. Anything you share with them is covered by their own privacy policies. We don't receive anything for listing them.

Children

Break-Up Kit isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Not medical care

Break-Up Kit is a self-help tool. It isn't therapy, medical care or a crisis service. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 in the US, or call 911 if you're in immediate danger.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. If a change ever means the app collects more data, we'll update this page before that version of the app is released.
